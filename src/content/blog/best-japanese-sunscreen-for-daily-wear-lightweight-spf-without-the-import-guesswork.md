## FAQ

### Is Japanese sunscreen better than drugstore SPF?

Not automatically. Some Japanese formulas feel lighter, which can make daily use easier. Coverage still depends on applying enough, choosing a formula you tolerate, and reapplying when the day runs long. A verified US fluid can be the better weekday pick if you will actually use it.

### What does PA++++ mean on Japanese sunscreen?

PA ratings are a UVA-protection clue used on many Japanese labels. They are useful shopping information. They are not a reason to apply a thinner layer or skip reapplication.

### Why does my Japanese sunscreen pill?

Common reasons include stacking too many serums, applying SPF before the last layer dries, using a silicone-heavy primer too soon, or rubbing instead of pressing. Simplify the morning and test the sunscreen alone for a few days.

### Can I use Japanese sunscreen under makeup?

Many people do. Let the SPF set first. If makeup slides or pills, the issue may be amount, wait time, or the moisturizer under it, not only the country on the tube.

### How do I know if an imported listing is a bad buy?

Look at recent reviews, seller information, packaging photos, expiration clues, and whether the product name matches the bottle. If those details are hard to find, compare another listing.
