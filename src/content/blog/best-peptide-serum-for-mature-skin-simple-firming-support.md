## What to look for on the label

Peptide labels can be confusing because brands use different names. You do not need to memorize every ingredient. Start with the product page, then check whether the formula makes sense for your skin and routine.

Helpful label clues include:

- Peptide, multi-peptide, copper peptide, or signal peptide wording
- Hydrating ingredients such as glycerin or hyaluronic acid
- Lightweight serum or gel-serum texture
- Fragrance-free if your skin reacts to scent
- Clear use directions for morning or night
- Packaging that keeps the product easy to dispense
- Reviews that mention layering under moisturizer or sunscreen

Be careful with:

- Strong fragrance if your skin is reactive
- Products that combine too many strong actives in one bottle
- Sticky textures if sunscreen already pills on you
- Dramatic firming promises that sound instant
- Buying several peptide products before testing one

Mature skin does not need the loudest serum. It needs a formula you can apply in a thin layer, cover with moisturizer, and repeat without negotiating every morning.

## Where peptide serum fits in the routine

Peptide serum usually sits after cleansing and before moisturizer. The exact placement depends on texture, but a thin serum normally goes before creams and sunscreen.

A simple morning order:

1. Cleanse or rinse gently.
2. Apply peptide serum in a thin layer.
3. Let it settle while you brush teeth or pack a bag.
4. Apply moisturizer where your skin needs comfort.
5. Apply sunscreen generously.
6. Use makeup only after SPF has settled.

A simple night order:

1. Remove sunscreen and makeup gently.
2. Cleanse without scrubbing.
3. Apply peptide serum, unless it conflicts with another treatment that night.
4. Follow with moisturizer.
5. Keep richer cream to dry zones if needed.

The routine should still feel repeatable. If adding a serum means you skip moisturizer or rush sunscreen, the new step is not helping the day.

## Texture matters more than the fanciest claim

The best serum on paper can fail if the texture annoys you. Mature skin routines often involve moisturizer, sunscreen, and maybe makeup, so the serum has to behave underneath those layers.

Look for a texture that:

- Spreads without tugging
- Absorbs without leaving a heavy film
- Does not pill under moisturizer
- Does not make sunscreen roll
- Feels comfortable by midday
- Works with the amount you can realistically apply

If a serum feels sticky, try using less before giving up. If it still pills, test it at night. If it only works when the rest of the routine is perfect, it may not be the right weekday serum.
