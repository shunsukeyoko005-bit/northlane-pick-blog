## How to layer drugstore SPF

Keep the order short enough to repeat.

Morning:

1. Rinse or cleanse gently.
2. Use a hydrating serum only if you already know it sits cleanly under SPF.
3. Moisturize dry zones if they need it. Skip a heavy all-over cream if that is what made you hate SPF.
4. Apply a full drugstore sunscreen layer to face, neck, ears, and the hairline you can reach.
5. Wait a short minute before glasses, a mask, or makeup if the schedule allows.

Night:

1. Remove sunscreen without scrubbing.
2. Keep the evening routine separate. A morning drugstore SPF is not a night treatment.

If I have a loud morning, I skip optional serums and keep moisturizer plus a full SPF. The drugstore bottle earns its spot only if I can finish that amount and buy another one without a hunt.
