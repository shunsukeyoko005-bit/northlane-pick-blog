## What to read on the label

"After retinol" is not a legal SPF category. The label still has to do ordinary sunscreen work.

Useful clues:

1. **Broad spectrum.** UVA and UVB both matter on a weekday, not only a beach day.
2. **SPF number you will actually use.** SPF 30 or 50 is the usual shopping range. A higher number is not a reason to apply less.
3. **Texture.** Fluid, lotion, or cream. Pick the one you can spread on slightly dry skin without rubbing raw.
4. **Fragrance.** Scent can make a retinol-dry morning harder to read.
5. **Finish under makeup.** If flakes are already there, a thick paste can pill.
6. **Reapply plan.** A morning layer still wears down. A stick in the car helps more than a second night product.

Do not treat "clean" or "natural" as proof it will feel easy on retinol skin.

## Mistakes that make after-retinol SPF less useful

1. **Skipping SPF because last night felt thorough.** Night and morning do different jobs.
2. **Applying too little because skin feels dry.** Thin texture is not permission to dab.
3. **Putting SPF on a stripped, stinging face.** Add moisturizer first, or pause the retinol night.
4. **Changing retinol and sunscreen in the same week.** You will not know which step bothered you.
5. **Using a stick as the whole face at 7 a.m.** Most weekday faces do not get enough that way.
6. **Judging sting in the first five seconds, then quitting the habit.** Give the moisturizer-plus-SPF pair a few weekday tests.

The smaller fix is often better: one familiar moisturizer, one sunscreen you will finish, and retinol only as often as your mornings stay wearable.

## A simple testing plan

1. Choose one morning sunscreen.
2. Read the current label for SPF, broad spectrum, fragrance, and texture.
3. Keep your retinol schedule the same. Do not raise frequency while you test SPF.
4. Use moisturizer, then SPF, for several weekday mornings.
5. Watch for sting, tightness, pilling, cast, or a skipped day because the texture felt like work.
6. Reapply on the nose and cheeks if you are outside again.
7. Continue only if your skin feels steady and you will actually wear it.
