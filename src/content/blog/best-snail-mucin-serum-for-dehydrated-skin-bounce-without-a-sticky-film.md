## FAQ

### Do I need snail mucin if I already use hyaluronic acid?

Not always. If a hyaluronic acid serum plus moisturizer keeps your skin comfortable, you may not need another hydration bottle. Snail mucin is worth comparing when you want a more cushiony slip than a watery serum, and when you can keep it to one layer.

### Can snail mucin replace moisturizer?

Usually no. Most mucin serums add a water-like or film-like comfort layer, then still want a cream or lotion after. Dehydrated skin that feels tight by lunch is often missing the seal, not a higher filtrate percentage.

### Why does snail mucin feel sticky?

A thick texture, too much product, or not enough wait time before the next step can leave a tacky film. Try a smaller amount on damp skin, then moisturizer. If it still feels gluey, a gel-hybrid or night-only use may fit better.

### Can I use snail mucin under sunscreen?

Yes if the layer is thin and settled. If it pills, use less, wait longer, or keep mucin for night and use a simpler morning stack.

### Is a 96% snail mucin bottle better than a mixed serum?

Not automatically. A high-filtrate bottle can feel cushiony, and it can also stay tacky. A mixed gel may be easier on weekday mornings. Compare texture and finish, not only the number on the front.

### How often should I use it while testing?

Start once a day for a week, morning or night. If skin stays comfortable and the finish behaves under your other products, you can decide whether both times of day are worth it.
