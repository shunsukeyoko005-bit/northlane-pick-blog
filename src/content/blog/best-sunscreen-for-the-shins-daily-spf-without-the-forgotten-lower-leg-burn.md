## What to skip while testing shin sunscreen

Make the test boring for a week. Shins give clearer feedback when you change one variable at a time.

Avoid starting a new sunscreen beside:

- A new scented body lotion
- A new exfoliating body wash on the same area
- A gritty scrub on dry shins
- A heavy oil right before sunscreen
- Scrubbing hard to remove mineral cast
- Testing three sunscreens on the same shin in one day
- Sitting on a dark seat before the layer dries
- Skipping the sides and covering only the front bone

If the sunscreen fails, you want to know whether it was the formula, the moisturizer underneath, or the fabric that rubbed it off before you left the driveway.
