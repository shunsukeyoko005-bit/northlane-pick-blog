## What to look for in sunscreen after exfoliating

The label cannot promise comfort for every face, but it can give you useful clues before you buy.

Helpful clues include:

1. **Broad spectrum SPF 30 or higher.** The daily protection claim still matters more than a pretty finish.
2. **Texture you can apply generously.** A comfortable fluid, lotion, gel-cream, or mineral cream beats a formula you dab on too thinly.
3. **Fragrance awareness.** Perfume, fragrance, essential oils, and strong scent can make a post-active test harder to read.
4. **Moisture-support language.** Glycerin, dimethicone, ceramides, panthenol, hyaluronic acid, and similar ingredients can help the step feel less bare.
5. **Low-rub application.** A sunscreen that spreads without dragging is useful when skin feels freshly exfoliated.
6. **Compatibility with your moisturizer.** Pilling can come from the layer underneath, not only the sunscreen.

The goal is not the most elegant finish on a perfect-skin day. The goal is sunscreen you will still apply enough of after an exfoliation night.

## A simple morning routine after exfoliating

Keep this short enough for a real weekday.

Morning:

1. Rinse or cleanse gently, depending on what your skin usually tolerates.
2. Skip scrubs, peel pads, and extra active steps.
3. Apply a light moisturizer if skin feels tight.
4. Apply broad spectrum SPF 30 or higher in a generous layer.
5. Let it settle before makeup, glasses, or a mask if your schedule gives you a minute.

If the whole routine takes too long, remove the optional extras first. Cleanser, moisturizer if needed, and sunscreen are the pieces to protect.

## What to skip the morning after exfoliating

The morning after exfoliating is a bad time to make the routine louder.

For a clearer sunscreen test, skip:

- A second exfoliating acid because skin still looks dull
- A scrubby cleanser
- A strong vitamin C serum if your skin already feels warm
- A new retinol or retinal product
- Fragranced toner under sunscreen
- Several hydrating layers that may pill
- Rubbing sunscreen off and starting over three times
- Testing a brand-new sunscreen on the same morning as a brand-new exfoliant

This is not about being precious with skincare. It is about making one change readable before you spend more money.
