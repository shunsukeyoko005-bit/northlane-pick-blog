## What to check before buying

Start with boring details before the texture hype. They tell you whether the sunscreen can become a repeatable daily product.

Useful checks include:

1. **SPF and broad spectrum positioning.** Look for SPF 30 or higher for daily face use, and read how the product describes UVA coverage.
2. **Current seller and listing clarity.** Imported skincare can move through different sellers, so check recent reviews, seller reputation, and packaging photos.
3. **Expiration or freshness details.** Sunscreen is not a forever product. Avoid mystery listings that make the date hard to understand.
4. **Texture language.** Gel, essence, watery, milk, cream, lotion, and balm all feel different under moisturizer and makeup.
5. **Fragrance notes.** If scent bothers your skin or your morning, make fragrance-free or low-scent wording part of the filter.
6. **Reapplication reality.** A pretty finish still needs enough product and touch-ups when the day runs long.

The goal is not to become a sunscreen detective. It is to avoid buying a beautiful tube you cannot confidently use, reorder, or layer.

## How to layer Korean sunscreen in a short morning routine

Keep the routine plain enough that sunscreen still happens when the house is loud.

1. Cleanse gently or rinse if your skin does not need a full wash.
2. Apply one serum only if it already earns its place.
3. Moisturize dry zones with a thin layer.
4. Apply Korean sunscreen generously to face, neck, ears, and exposed chest.

1. Remove sunscreen without scrubbing.
2. Cleanse gently.
3. Moisturize before skin feels tight.
4. Keep stronger actives familiar while testing the new SPF.
