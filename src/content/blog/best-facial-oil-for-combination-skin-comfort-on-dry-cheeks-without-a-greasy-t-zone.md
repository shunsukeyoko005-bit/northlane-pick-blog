## What to check before buying

You do not need a chemistry chart before coffee. A few listing clues make the first pass easier.

Helpful checks include:

1. **Simple oil names.** Squalane and jojoba are easier first tests than a long botanical blend.
2. **Lightweight or fast-absorbing wording.** Combination skin usually wants a thin oil, not a salad-dressing slip.
3. **Dropper packaging.** A dropper is easier to dose than a wide-mouth bottle when you only want two drops for cheeks.
4. **Fragrance-free or low-scent options.** Scent does not prove an oil is "natural" or "better." It can make a weekday test harder to live with.
5. **Non-comedogenic language.** This is not a guarantee. It is a shopping clue to compare, then judge by your own T-zone after a few days.
6. **Directions that allow spot use.** If the listing only talks about massaging a full-face layer, you can still use less. The habit matters more than the marketing photo.
7. **Review language about greasy, shiny, pores, or dry patches.** That is more useful than "luxurious glow" first-day notes.
8. **Current listing details.** Formulas, sellers, and directions change, so read the product page before buying.

The goal is not a bottle that makes every zone identical. It is an oil you can put on dry cheeks in 20 seconds and leave off a shiny forehead.

## FAQ

### Can combination skin use facial oil?

Yes, combination skin can use a facial oil if you keep the amount small and put it on dry areas first. The usual problem is all-over application, not the idea of oil itself.

### Will facial oil make my T-zone oilier?

It can look shinier if you put oil on a zone that already looks slick, or if leftover oil travels. Start with cheeks only. If the T-zone looks worse after a few days, use less or keep oil for night.

### Should I use facial oil in the morning?

You can, but many weekday routines are easier with oil at night. Morning oil under sunscreen is a common reason SPF pills. If you try it in the morning, use less, wait, then apply sunscreen.

### Is squalane better than jojoba for combination skin?

Neither is automatically better. Squalane is a simple first test. Jojoba is another common simple oil. Texture, amount, and where you apply it matter more than the trendy name on the dropper.

### Do I still need moisturizer if I use facial oil?

Usually yes. Oil is a finishing step, not a full stand-in for moisturizer or a hydrating serum. If cheeks still feel tight, hydrate and moisturize first, then add oil only where needed.

### Can I use facial oil with niacinamide or retinol?

Many people use a simple oil in a routine that already includes other products, but do not start several new bottles in the same week. Keep the oil test quiet. If skin stings, pauses, or looks irritated, stop stacking and reassess.
