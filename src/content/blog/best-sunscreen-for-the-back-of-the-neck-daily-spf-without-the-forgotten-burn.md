The nape sits behind you. It does not show up in a bathroom mirror the way cheeks do. Hair covers it until a clip, ponytail, or wind moves that cover. Collars, seatbelts, and bags also rub the same strip all day.

The fix is not a ten-step neck routine. It is a better sequence: apply your main SPF, add a small second pass from the hairline down to the collar, then carry a realistic touch-up format for days when hair goes up later.

## What to look for in sunscreen for the nape

Start with a sunscreen you can use on the face or upper body, then treat the back of the neck as a high-wear edge.

The goal is not a separate product for every body part. It is to stop pretending one rushed face swipe covers the strip that hair, collars, and sidelight keep finding.

## What to skip while testing a new nape-friendly SPF

Make the sunscreen test boring on purpose.

For the first week or two, avoid starting it beside:

- A new exfoliating acid
- A new retinoid or retinal
- A strong vitamin C serum
- A fragranced cleanser
- A new hair oil or spray on the same skin
- Aggressive scrubbing to remove sunscreen
- Several new "calming" products in the same cart

This is not about making skincare precious. It is about knowing whether the sunscreen was comfortable, or whether the whole routine was too loud.
