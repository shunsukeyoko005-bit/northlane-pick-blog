## What to look for on the label

Start with the back of the bottle and the texture claims, not the prettiest "glass skin" phrase on the listing.

Helpful clues include:

1. **Snow mushroom, tremella, or tremella fuciformis wording.** Make sure the ingredient is actually part of the formula story.
2. **Humectant support.** Glycerin, hyaluronic acid, panthenol, beta-glucan, aloe, and similar comfort ingredients can make the serum feel more complete.
3. **Fragrance-free if scent bothers you.** Dehydrated-feeling skin can be hard to read when fragrance is part of the test.
4. **A texture you can use under SPF.** Watery, gel, or light serum texture usually matters more than a dramatic ingredient list.
5. **Limited active crowding.** Strong acids, retinoids, and high-strength brightening blends can make the first test noisy.
6. **Simple directions.** If the bottle needs several layers and a special routine, it may not fit a busy morning.

Be careful with formulas that try to be a hydrating serum, exfoliating treatment, brightening peel, and fragrant spa step in one bottle. That is a lot to troubleshoot when all you wanted was skin that feels less tight.
